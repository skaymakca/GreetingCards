# Tips

- OCR and AI results are cached in the database — reloading the same files is instant
- AI analysis gives more accurate results than OCR alone, especially for handwritten cards
- Manual edits override all automatic extractions and are shown with a blue dot
- Use <kbd>Option</kbd>-click on sidebar filters to select multiple categories at once
- Right-click a name field and choose **Title Case** to fix capitalization
- Use **Reset All Card Data** in **Settings** &rsaquo; **Advanced** to erase all manual entries, candidates, cached OCR, and cached AI results
- You can load cards from multiple folders — they accumulate rather than replace
