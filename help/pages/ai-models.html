AI Models

Greeting Cards uses Claude AI to analyze card images and extract family names. You can choose which model to use based on your needs for speed, quality, and cost.

Available Models

Model Speed Quality Est. Cost per Card*
Claude Haiku 4.5 Fastest Good ~0.4¢
Claude Sonnet 4.6 Fast Excellent ~1.3¢
Claude Opus 4.6 Moderate Best ~2.1¢

*Estimates based on a typical 2-page greeting card. Actual costs depend on image size and content.

Choosing a Model

  • Haiku 4.5 — Best for large batches where speed and cost matter most. Handles most cards well.
  • Sonnet 4.6 — Recommended default. Good balance of accuracy and cost for everyday use.
  • Opus 4.6 — Best for difficult-to-read cards with unusual handwriting or low contrast.

How to Change

  1. Open Settings (Cmd+,)
  2. In the General tab, find the AI Model dropdown
  3. Select your preferred model

The change takes effect immediately for new AI analyses. Cards that have already been analyzed are not re-processed automatically.
