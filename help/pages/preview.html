Preview Panel

Viewing Cards

  • Select a card in the list to view it in the preview panel
  • Multi-page cards show navigation arrows and a page indicator
  • Use Left / Right arrow keys to navigate pages

Pan and Zoom

  • Click and drag to pan when zoomed in
  • Scroll wheel to zoom in and out at cursor position
  • Shift + Click — Zoom in
  • Option + Click — Zoom out
  • Use the + and − buttons for precise zoom control
  • Fit button — Reset to fit window (never exceeds actual size)
