Keyboard Shortcuts

General

  • Cmd+O — Open files or folders
  • Cmd+Shift+I — AI Analyze All
  • Cmd+R — Rename Files
  • Cmd+, — Open Settings
  • Cmd+F — Focus search/filter field
  • Esc — Defocus text entries or clear search

Navigation

  • Up / Down — Navigate cards in list
  • Shift+Up / Shift+Down — Extend card selection
  • Left / Right — Navigate pages in preview

Selection

  • Cmd+A — Select all cards
  • Cmd+Shift+A — Select none
  • Cmd+Delete — Remove selected cards

Editing

  • Cmd+X — Cut
  • Cmd+C — Copy
  • Cmd+V — Paste

Zoom Controls

  • Shift + Click — Zoom in (cursor shows +)
  • Option + Click — Zoom out (cursor shows −)
  • Scroll Wheel — Zoom in/out at cursor
  • Fit button — Reset to fit window
