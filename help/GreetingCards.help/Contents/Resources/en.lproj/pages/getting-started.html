Getting Started

Workflow

  1. Click Add Files/Folders or drag-drop PDFs/folders to load cards
  2. Cards are automatically processed with OCR to extract family names
  3. Review extracted names in the cards list (filter by confidence level)
  4. Click the AI button (per card or AI All) for better accuracy
  5. Edit names manually or select from the Candidates dropdown
  6. Click Rename All to rename files with extracted names
Note: You can load cards from multiple folders! They accumulate rather than replacing previous loads. Identical files at different locations are automatically detected and merged.
