Greeting Cards

Organize your greeting cards with OCR and AI

Drop a folder of scanned greeting cards, and Greeting Cards extracts family names using offline OCR and Claude AI vision, then batch-renames every file into a clean, consistent format — no manual typing required.

macOS · Free & open source

Features

Offline OCR

Tesseract-powered text extraction works entirely offline. Your cards never leave your Mac.

AI Vision

Claude analyzes card images directly — catching names that OCR misses in decorative fonts and handwriting.

Batch Rename

One click renames all your cards into a clean, consistent format with family names and occasions.

Dark Mode

Follows your macOS appearance automatically. Every panel, dialog, and preview adapts seamlessly.

AppleScript

Fully scriptable via Apple Events. Automate card processing from shell scripts or Shortcuts.

Auto-Update

Built-in Sparkle updater keeps you current. New versions install seamlessly in the background.
